## Hooks and settings

The shared `.claude/settings.json` allows common read and build commands, denies destructive ones, asks before `gh pr merge` and `gh workflow run`, and wires two hooks: [`guard-env-branches.mjs`](https://github.com/enhancedai-com/Workflow/blob/main/.claude/hooks/guard-env-branches.mjs) (no commits on env branches, no merging `develop` or `staging` into a feature branch, no force push, no push to `main`) and [`guard-dev-loop.mjs`](https://github.com/enhancedai-com/Workflow/blob/main/.claude/hooks/guard-dev-loop.mjs) (plan, approval and frozen tests on task branches). See [Guardrails](/ai-workflows/claude-code-guardrails).
