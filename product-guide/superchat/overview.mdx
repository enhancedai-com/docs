---
title: "SuperChat"
description: "SuperChat is the assistant built into FluxPrompt that answers questions about your agents, folders, spaces and organization."
---

SuperChat is the chat assistant built into FluxPrompt. You ask it about your workspace, and it answers using what FluxPrompt knows about your agents, folders, spaces and organization: their companion documents and live facts about them.

Every agent, folder, space and organization has a companion document that describes what it is for and what should not change without asking. SuperChat reads these documents before it answers or acts, so keeping them accurate makes its answers better. You can read and edit a companion document in the app, or over the [Integrations API](/main-api/specific-features/integrations-api/companion-docs).

Your conversations are kept per user. You can rename or delete a conversation, and stop an answer while it is being written. Whether SuperChat is available to you depends on your account's access, and daily usage is capped.

The pages in this group explain what SuperChat can do and how to get good answers from it.
