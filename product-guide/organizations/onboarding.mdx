## What it is

There are two ways to start:

| Path | For | How it starts |
|------|-----|---------------|
| **Try it first** | Anyone visiting [app.fluxprompt.ai](https://app.fluxprompt.ai) | You describe a task, FluxPrompt builds an agent and runs it — before you create an account. |
| **Set up with the FluxPrompt team** | Organizations onboarding with a FluxPrompt account manager | The FluxPrompt team creates your organization, spaces, members and plan during your onboarding call. You receive an email invitation. |

## Try it first (no sign-up)

### Who can use it

Anyone who is not signed in. Nothing to install and no credit card.

### Step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe a task">
    Open [app.fluxprompt.ai](https://app.fluxprompt.ai). In the box **Describe how something gets done in
    your business...**, type what you want done, or click one of the examples. Submit with **Build it**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="FluxPrompt builds the agent">
    You see **Setting up your workspace…** and **No signup yet. We are building the agent first.** The agent
    opens in the editor. If a custom build takes too long, FluxPrompt starts you from a starter template and
    tells you so.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill what is missing and add your email">
    The panel next to the agent asks for anything the agent still needs, and for an email in
    **Email the result to…**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it">
    Click **Run it now**. When the agent finishes you see **It ran.**, and a copy of the result is emailed to
    you with the subject *Your agent ran: (agent name)*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your agent">
    To change the agent or keep going, FluxPrompt asks you to create an account: **Save your agent to keep
    going**. Sign up with a social account, **Continue with email**, or choose **I already have an account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What you get

- Before you add an email, you have a small preview allowance of credits.
- Adding your email unlocks the free credits of the **Intro** plan.
- A new account starts on the **Intro** plan with a personal workspace, and receives the email
  *Your FluxPrompt workspace is ready*.

### Limits

- The preview allowance is small. When it runs out you see **That is the end of the preview. Add your email
  to unlock your free FluxTokens and keep going.**
- Only a limited number of preview sessions can be started from the same network per hour.
- Until you create an account, you can run the agent but not ask for changes to it.

### Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| **We could not start your agent. Please try again.** | Try again. If it keeps failing, click **Get Started Free** and sign up first. |
| **That email already has an account. Sign in to keep going.** | Your email already has a FluxPrompt account. Sign in instead. |
| **Add an email so we can send you the result.** | Fill **Email the result to…** before you click **Run it now**. |
| **Too many sessions started from here. Sign in, or try again later.** | Too many previews were started from your network. Sign in, or wait an hour. |
| **That is the end of the preview. Add your email to unlock your free FluxTokens and keep going.** | Add your email to keep going. |

## Set up with the FluxPrompt team

### Who can use it

Organizations that onboard with a FluxPrompt account manager. The FluxPrompt team prepares everything during
your onboarding call; you do not need to create the organization yourself.

### What the FluxPrompt team sets up

- Your **organization**, with you (the main contact) as its **Owner**.
- Your **spaces**. You are an admin of each of them.
- Your teammates, each with a role: **Admin**, **Builder** or **User** (see
  [Roles and permissions](/product-guide/organizations/roles-and-permissions)).
- Your **plan**, including any terms agreed in your contract.

### Step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation">
    Every member receives an email with the subject **Company invitation**, inviting them to join the
    organization in FluxPrompt. Click **Click here to accept the invite**. The link is valid for **7 days**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your password">
    If you are new to FluxPrompt, choose a password, accept the Terms of Service and click
    **Continue to plan**. If you already have an account, you accept the invitation with it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Owner: activate the plan">
    The Owner sees **YOU'VE BEEN INVITED** and three steps: **Account**, **Plan** and **Payment**. Review
    **Your plan** and click **Continue to payment** to add a payment method.
    If your contract is billed outside the app, the page says **Billed per your agreement**: you only confirm
    a card, which is verified and not charged (**Confirm card**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Everyone else: a short tour">
    Other members see a short introduction to FluxPrompt, then land in the organization.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Before payment

Until the Owner completes payment, **Billing** shows **• Pending activation**, and the organization runs on
a limited credit allowance. Completing payment activates the plan and its included credits.

### Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| The invitation link does not work | Links expire after 7 days. Ask your account manager to send it again. |
| **There is no Enterprise plan waiting for a card on this account.** | Sign in with the Owner's account, or contact your account manager. |
| **This Enterprise plan has no negotiated terms yet. Contact your account manager.** | Your account manager still has to finish setting up your plan. |
| **We could not verify this card.** | Try another card. |
| Credits run out quickly before activation | The organization is on a limited allowance until the plan is paid. |
