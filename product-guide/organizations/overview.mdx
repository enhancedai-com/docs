---
title: "Organizations and roles"
description: "How organizations, spaces and roles decide who can build, change and run agents in FluxPrompt."
---

An organization is the account your team shares in FluxPrompt. Inside it, work is split into spaces, and each space holds folders and agents.

Every member of an organization has one organization role:

| Role | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Owner | Owns the organization. Has every admin permission. |
| Admin | Manages members, spaces and settings. |
| Builder | Creates and changes agents. |
| User | Uses the agents others built. |

Inside a space, a member is either a space **admin** or a space **member**. A person sees only the spaces they belong to, so adding someone to an organization usually also means adding them to the spaces they work in.

The pages in this group explain how to invite people, change roles and organize spaces.
